# workout-dumbbell-concept2 — abandoned

Fork-on-top compression redesign of `workout-dumbbell-holder` v3.4 (fork plate moved above the flange, tilted 15° outboard-up, single curved-arc strut). Modeled in Fusion 360 to 182 cm³ on 2026-05-10 but never printed.

Abandoned because the redesign was no longer useful — v3.4 covers the actual workout use case adequately, and the compression-load structural argument didn't outweigh the cost of a second SKU. Kept here for the Fusion construction notes (Matrix3D rotation workaround for tilted plates, YZ-sketch coord convention) in case a future tilted-fork design wants the recipe.

See `output/modeling-report.md` for the build journal.
